# Simple.solar — niche research (October 22, 2026)

44 solar-niche websites were reviewed for features, forms, content and design. Six fetches were blocked or failed (MarketWatch Guides, Angi /solar, the Modernize quote flow, Solar Panel Talk, pmsuryaghar.gov.in, Verbraucherzentrale); search snippets or sister pages stood in for those.

## Sites reviewed
**Marketplaces / lead gen:** EnergySage (marketplace + calculator), SolarReviews, Solar.com, Modernize, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Solar Choice (AU), NRG Clean Power quick quote, Sunrun, Tesla Solar, SunPower.
**Publishers:** CleanTechnica, Solar Power World, pv magazine (plus its plug-in coverage), TaiyangNews, Electrek, Forbes Home, ConsumerAffairs, This Old House, MarketWatch Guides, Which? (UK).
**Tools / data / government:** NREL PVWatts, Google Project Sunroof, DSIRE, SEIA, IRENA, Global Solar Atlas, US DOE Homeowner's Guide to Going Solar, PM Surya Ghar (India).
**DIY / portable / off-grid:** ShopSolarKits, Renogy, Signature Solar, Unbound Solar (formerly Wholesale Solar), A1SolarStore, EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker SOLIX (US + DE), Bluetti, WillApproved (Will Prowse), Off Grid Ham, Bright Saver (plug-in solar), Finanztip (DE balcony solar), NEF (formerly YouGen UK).

## What the best sites share (ranked by impact)
1. A single-field hero entry (ZIP or address) above the fold.
2. A useful estimate shown *before* asking for contact details.
3. Programmatic state/city pages (cost, incentives, payback).
4. Cost pages that lead with a range and an average.
5. Comparison tables with a highlighted recommendation.
6. Named authors, methodology and editorial standards.
7. Trust bars: scale stats, ratings, press mentions, "no spam calls".
8. A numbered "how it works" sequence.
9. Use-case navigation (home, renter, RV, cabin, backup).
10. Red-flag / scam checklists.
11. FAQ blocks with question-style headings; dated "2026" titles.
12. Runtime/power-needs calculators for portable gear.
13. Newsletter capture; repeated CTA after each major section.
14. Affiliate and partner disclosures.
15. Legal trackers for new categories (plug-in solar).

## Highest-converting lead-form pattern (built into /quotes.html)
ZIP → own or rent (renters routed to plug-in/community solar instead of being dropped) → monthly bill → roof sun/shade → battery interest → timeline + payment preference → instant estimate shown → name, email, phone → choose how many installers contact you (up to 3, or email only) → TCPA-style consent ("not a condition of purchase") → "See my estimate".

## Gaps Simple.solar owns
- One decision router across rooftop, battery, plug-in, portable and RV solar (the "Which solar fits me?" quiz).
- Renter and apartment solar (every quote site disqualifies renters).
- State-by-state plug-in solar status in plain English.
- Brand-neutral power-station sizing ("what will it actually run?").
- Quote-reading help ($/W, dealer fees, escalators, red flags).
- Batteries without the upsell: when they pay under your export rules.
- A plain-English glossary.

## Design cues observed
Navy/indigo bases with warm orange CTAs dominate; most heroes are rooftop photos with a ZIP box. Simple.solar deliberately differs: an illustrated sun-arc calculator as the hero, a yolk-yellow accent, and a highly legible type pairing (Bricolage Grotesque + Atkinson Hyperlegible).
