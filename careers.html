How we work

  • Remote-first, asynchronous, with clear briefs and fair deadlines.
  • Paid on time. Rates are shared up front, before any work starts.
  • Every guide is fact-checked and lists its data sources.
  • We welcome career changers, students and retirees with field experience.

Hiring process

  1. Send the form.
  2. We reply within five business days.
  3. A short paid test task for writing and video roles.
  4. Onboarding call and first assignment.

We will never ask you to pay for training, equipment or a background check. Treat any such request in our name as fraud.
