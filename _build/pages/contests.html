Enter a contest

Official rules, in short

  • No purchase necessary. Buying anything does not improve your chances.
  • Eligibility. Open to legal residents of the US and Canada aged 18+, except where prohibited. The student challenge requires parental consent for minors.
  • Judging. A panel scores entries on clarity (40%), usefulness to other readers (40%) and originality (20%). Skill-based; no random draws.
  • Dates. Each contest runs for one calendar quarter. Entries close at 11:59 pm Eastern on the last day of the quarter; winners are announced within 30 days.
  • Your rights. You keep ownership of your work and grant Simple.solar a non-exclusive license to publish it with credit.
  • Prizes. Not transferable. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes. Canadian winners must answer a skill-testing question.
  • Privacy. Entry details are used only to run the contest. See our privacy policy.

Contest questions

Can I enter more than one contest?

Yes. One entry per person per contest per quarter.

Do I need a big system to win the showcase?

No. A well-explained 400 W balcony setup can beat a 15 kW roof. We judge how useful your story is to other readers.

How are winners contacted?

By email from Simple.solar within 30 days of the contest closing. We never ask winners for payment or banking passwords.
