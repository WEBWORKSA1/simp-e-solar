Partner standards

  • Licensed in every state you serve, with current general liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  • Written workmanship warranty of at least 10 years.
  • No high-pressure tactics, no "free solar" claims, and honest production estimates that reflect shade and orientation.
  • Clear pricing that shows total cost, price per watt, and any dealer fee in loan offers.
  • Respond to new requests within one business day and honor opt-outs immediately.
