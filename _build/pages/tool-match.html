Rooftop solar

You own your roof and want long-term savings. A rooftop system offers the biggest payoff. In most states it pays back in 8 to 14 years and keeps producing for 25+.

Plug-in or balcony solar

A 400 to 1,200 W kit plugs into an outlet, costs hundreds rather than thousands, and moves with you. Check your state's rules first.

Solar plus a home battery

For reliable outage backup, pair panels with a 10 to 15 kWh battery and a critical-loads panel. Batteries also pay off where exports earn little.

A portable power station

No install, no permission needed. A 1 to 3 kWh station keeps a fridge, Wi-Fi and lights going through most outages, and folding panels recharge it.

RV or van solar

Size panels and a lithium battery to your daily watt-hours. Most part-time setups land between 200 and 600 W of panels.
