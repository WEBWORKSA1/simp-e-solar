Community guidelines

  • Be kind and specific. Share real numbers when you can.
  • No sales pitches or referral links in submissions.
  • Safety first: we won't publish wiring advice that skips a licensed electrician for panel connections.
  • Corrections are always welcome. Use the contact form.
