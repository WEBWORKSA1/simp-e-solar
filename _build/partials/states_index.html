How to read this table

Power price matters most. Every kWh your panels make is worth what you'd otherwise pay, so a home in a 30¢ state earns twice as much per panel as one in a 15¢ state.

Sun hours come second. A kW of panels in Arizona makes roughly 60% more energy than the same kW in Washington.

Export rules are the hidden third factor. Some utilities credit exported power at the full retail rate; others pay much less. See net metering explained.
