Trusted solar resources

Independent tools and data we use and recommend. None of these links are paid.

Estimate production for your exact address

  • NREL PVWatts — the industry-standard free production calculator from the National Renewable Energy Laboratory.
  • Global Solar Atlas — solar resource for any point on Earth, from the World Bank and Solargis.

Find incentives

  • DSIRE — the most complete database of US state, local and utility incentives.
  • IRS Residential Clean Energy Credit — official rules, including the end date for homeowner systems.
  • Natural Resources Canada — federal home energy programs.
  • Ofgem Smart Export Guarantee — UK export payments.
  • Clean Energy Regulator (Australia) — STCs and the Cheaper Home Batteries program.
  • PM Surya Ghar (India) — rooftop solar subsidy portal.

Consumer protection

  • US DOE Homeowner's Guide to Going Solar
  • FTC: solar power for your home — scams and contract warnings.
  • SEIA — industry data and the solar consumer protection guide.

Prices and market data

  • EIA Electric Power Monthly — official electricity prices by state.
  • Berkeley Lab Tracking the Sun — installed price trends from millions of systems.
  • IRENA — global renewable cost statistics.

Find a certified professional

  • NABCEP — the main North American solar installer certification. Look up certified pros.
