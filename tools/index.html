Where our numbers come from

Electricity prices come from the US Energy Information Administration's monthly residential averages. Sunshine comes from NREL's PVWatts and National Solar Radiation Database. Installed prices use a $3.00 per watt midpoint between EnergySage's 2026 marketplace data and SEIA/Wood Mackenzie's 2026 market report. See our editorial policy for how we update them.
