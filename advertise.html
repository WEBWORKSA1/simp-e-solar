Reach people deciding on solar

Our readers are mid-decision: comparing systems, sizing batteries and choosing between rooftop, plug-in and portable. Put your brand in front of them, clearly labeled and on topic.

Calculator sponsor

One sponsor per calculator, with a labeled placement beside the results.

Guide sponsor

Sponsor a guide cluster, such as batteries or portable power. Our editorial stays independent.

Newsletter

A featured slot in the monthly solar brief, sent to subscribers who opted in.

Contest prizes

Donate gear as a contest prize, with brand credit on the contest page and in winner announcements.

Our rules for advertising

  • Paid placements are always labeled "Sponsored" or "Advertisement".
  • Sponsors never see or edit guides before publication, and can't buy a ranking or a verdict.
  • No ads for products we consider unsafe, such as uncertified plug-in kits or panels sold with misleading wattage.
  • Display ads are served by Google AdSense and follow Google's publisher policies.

Good fits

Solar and battery manufacturers, portable power brands, installers and EPCs, solar lenders, EV chargers, home energy audits, smart thermostats, RV and outdoor brands, and climate-tech startups.
