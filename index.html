Questions people ask first

Is solar still worth it without the 30% federal tax credit?

Often, yes. The US homeowner tax credit ended for systems installed after 2025, which stretches payback by a few years. In states with high electricity prices and decent sun, many systems still pay for themselves in 8 to 12 years and keep saving for 25+. Run your own numbers in the savings calculator.

Can I go solar if I rent?

Yes. Plug-in panels, community solar subscriptions and portable power stations don't need a roof you own. See solar for renters.

Do solar panels work during a power outage?

Standard grid-tied systems shut off during an outage for the safety of utility workers. To keep power on you need a battery or a system designed to island. See outage backup options.

How long do solar panels last?

Most panels carry 25 to 30 year warranties and lose roughly 0.25% to 0.5% of output per year. Inverters usually need replacing once, around year 10 to 15.
